Settings are in data/cities_config.json (growth, pool, siege), people_per_strength is global until Task 38b moves it to the division type.

- Capture choices (Task 39; docs/specs/CAPTURE.md): taking a city opens a popup for the player (the AI uses ai_default_option, Annex, until Task 40) with Annex (-25% people, -30% infra, 26-turn rebuild, pool 0, 10-turn recruit lockout), Occupy (-10% people, labor x0.5, pool 0, no training, annexable free after 5 turns via a city panel button), Plunder (+5 cash per 1,000 people, -15% people, -50% infra, then annexed), Reduce (-50% people who flee to the old owner as refugees, -50% infra, then annexed), Raze (city removed, its land freed, 50% of its people flee to the old owner's nearest city, the rest die; allowed on a last city, which eliminates the nation, its armies surrendering as POWs) and Enslave (-10% people, city.enslaved: growth, pool and training 0, labor x1.5). Every choice logs a reputation_event (stub: HexMap.reputation, nothing reads it); Raze is always an atrocity, severity 3. All numbers are in data/cities_config.json "capture". Turn end waits on the popup (DivisionRoster.pending_captures).
- Capture gating and AI personality (Task 40; docs/specs/CAPTURE.md): each faction may only use the capture options in the cities_config.json capture.faction_options table (all may Annex; a gated option is disabled in the popup with a reason and falls back to Annex if forced). The AI takes its faction's harshest allowed option (capture.harshness_order) with probability aggressive_harsh_chance (0.5) if the faction is in capture.aggressive_factions (Directorate, Collective, Free Tide, Drift), else Annex; ai_default_option is only the fallback for a nation with no faction. Atrocity is judged per observer: each faction in factions.json has views_as_atrocity (Raze always on it); HexMap.is_atrocity_to(faction, event) and reputation_weight_to(faction, event) (capture.atrocity_weight x the faction's optional atrocity_sensitivity, default 1.0, else base severity) are for Task 42's attitude to consume, nothing does yet. The event's own atrocity flag and severity are unchanged. The city panel shows Recruitable 0 for occupied and enslaved cities.
- Main menu and game setup (Task 40b): the main scene is scenes/menu.tscn (scripts/ui/main_menu.gd) with New Game, Load Game (needs the save system, Task 40c) and Quit. New Game opens the setup screen: faction picker (every faction not marked playable:false in factions.json), map seed (Random button plus a text field, bad text gets a random seed) and AI nations (map_config.json setup: min_ai 1, max_ai 3, default 3, the range the map generator fits cleanly on 20x12), then Start. The choices go through GameSession and main.gd applies them over map_config.json. The game scene is still scenes/main.tscn.
- Save and load (Task 40c): one manual slot (user://savegame.sav) written from the Esc menu (Save Game), and an autosave (user://autosave.sav) at the end of every turn. Load Game on the main menu loads the newest of the two (disabled with no save). scripts/save_game.gd (SaveGame.capture / restore / state_hash / write / read) round-trips the map (ownership, terrain, cities with every capture, rebuild and pool state), divisions with orders, economy stocks, recruit queues and POWs, reputation events, the AI random stream and the turn; new systems add their state through the `extras` argument (fog of war in 40f; alien Pressure does not exist yet, so Task 45 must add it there). Esc now leaves a target mode, else cancels the selected unit's order, else opens the menu (Resume, Save Game, Main Menu, Quit).
- Domination victory and defeat (Task 40d): at the end of each turn (scripts/victory.gd) a nation holding at least `victory.domination_share` (map_config.json, 0.85) of all nation-owned cities wins. Cities of neutral nations (natives) and cities flagged native or alien_site count neither for an owner nor in the total. The player wins if it is that nation; the player loses on elimination (no cities) or when an AI nation dominates. The end screen (GameOverScreen) offers New Game (opens the setup screen) or Main Menu, blocks the game input, and removes the autosave so a finished game is not resumed.
- Colony pods (Task 40e): no way to found a city after landfall existed before this (the second starting city per nation is placed by the map generator; PIVOT.md's "extra colony pod" was never built). A city can now queue a colony pod through its one Training queue (City panel button or P): it draws `colonization.population_cost` (3,000) people from the city's population at queue time, needs `labor_turns` (15) Training labor-turns, and the city must keep `min_remaining_population` (5,000) afterwards. The pod is a unit (`colony_pod` template: move 2, attack 0, defense 1, no upkeep, never reinforced, cannot attack, dig in or capture a city). Found city (B, or the unit panel button) on a valid hex (land, own or unclaimed territory, at least `min_city_distance` (3) hexes from every city) makes an Outpost of `outpost_population` (3,000) and uses the pod up. The AI follows the same rules: with `ai_build_pods` it queues up to `ai_max_pods` (1) pods per nation when a valid site exists and a city can spare the people, and its pods walk to the nearest reachable site and found. PM defaults flagged in the PR: pod strength/org/defense numbers, no upkeep, Outpost infrastructure 10 (same as every city), ai_max_pods 1.
- Fog of war with line of sight (Task 40f; scripts/fog.gd, map_config.json `fog` and `ai`): no fog code existed anywhere in the repo, its history, docs or archive, so it is new. Three states per tile per nation: Unexplored (black), Explored (terrain and cities as last seen, with the owner and city tier at that time, dimmed, no units; the world keeps changing unseen) and Visible (live). At landfall only the 2 hexes around each starting city are explored (`landfall_radius`). Sight in hexes (`sight`): infantry 2, scout 3, colony pod 2 (PM default), any other unit 2, cities 2, +`city_sight_bonus` 1 at the City tier or higher (15,000 people, `tier_thresholds` from POPULATION_TUNING), +`elevated_sight_bonus` 1 for units on hills or mountains; `blocking_terrain` (mountains) blocks sight to the tiles behind it (hex line check; the mountain itself and a viewer's own tile never block). Enemy units draw only on Visible tiles, cannot be selected or targeted (`DivisionRoster.hides_from`, set_order and action_targets) in the fog; clicking an enemy city you cannot see shows "Last seen". The AI stays omniscient (`ai.respects_fog` false, flagged as a PM default; there is no AI fog logic, the flag only widens the same targeting filter). Explored memory is saved with the game (SaveGame extras "fog"); the visible set is recomputed. A full update takes about 1 ms on the test map.
- Scout (Task 40g; divisions_config.json `scout`): a cheap unarmed explorer. Recruited through the city's normal queue (City panel button or S): `recruit.people` 200 from the pool and population, `recruit.labor_turns` 5 of Training labor, no capacity cost, same pool and city-size rules as a division. Move 3, sight 3 (fog config), strength 20 (so 200 people at 10 per point), defense 1, attack 0, upkeep 1 cash. It cannot attack at all (`unarmed`, shared with colony pods) and cannot capture a city. `ignores_frontage_cap` is a flag only: the frontage cap (Task 46) does not exist yet and must read it. Fungus attrition (Task 44) does not exist yet either; the scout has no exemption (no `fungus_immune`), so it will apply. The AI queues `recruit.ai_count` (1) scout per nation on its first turn (before pods and divisions) and sends it to the nearest reachable tile its nation has never explored; fog is read only to choose where to look. Scouts count as divisions for the AI cap; queued scouts count at scout upkeep in the projected-upkeep check.

- Affinity, attitude and AI target weighting (Task 42; docs/specs/FACTION_TRAITS.md): every faction in factions.json has integer `axes` (order, faith, planet, -2 to +2, from the spec). `Attitude` (HexMap.attitude, `HexMap.attitude_between(a, b)`) = -(sum of the axis gaps) plus `global.rival_attitude` (-2) if b is in a's rivals (not symmetric), minus b's atrocity record as a sees it: every reputation_event b caused counts `HexMap.reputation_weight_to(a's faction, event)` (capture.atrocity_weight 3.0 x the observer's atrocity_sensitivity if the event is an atrocity to it, else the base severity) x `global.reputation_attitude_scale` (1.0); attitude(Assembly, Directorate) = -7, (Assembly, Free Tide) = 0. The AI (`DivisionRoster.run_ai`) scores each enemy city within `ai.target_range` (12): -attitude x `attitude_weight` - hex distance x `distance_weight` + (my local strength / their local strength - 1) x 10 x `strength_ratio_weight`, where local strength is the strength of a nation's divisions within `ai.local_radius` (2) and an empty city counts `ai.empty_city_strength` (1); it heads for the best score above `min_attack_score` (0), closing in beside a garrisoned city, and attacks the lowest-attitude adjacent enemy first (ties random); with no target it wanders or fortifies as before. Neutral nations and unarmed units are never targets. No diplomacy reactions, no decay of the atrocity record, no attitude UI yet. A map with no attitude data loaded (the tests' default) keeps the old AI.
- City tiers (Task 43; docs/specs/CITY_TIERS.md): tiers are a label plus claim radius, no stat effects. cities_config.json `tiers` (outpost 0, base 5,000, city 15,000 with claim_radius 2, metropolis 30,000 with claim_radius 2, each with a placeholder `sprite_scale` 0.6/0.8/1.0/1.2), `colony_start_population` 3,000 and `capital_start_population` 10,000 replace `start_population`; `grown_radius_population` and `grown_claim_radius` are gone (claim radius comes from the tier, default `claim_radius` 1; City tier is now 15,000, was 20,000). HexMap.tier(city) / tier_for_population / next_tier_population; tiers are recalculated at the end of each turn after growth and losses and a change goes to `HexMap.tier_changes` and the turn log (main.gd prints it, and shows the player's in the message bar), e.g. "City 3 grew to a City". PM ruling: a city that drops below City tier keeps the ring-2 tiles it held (`city.kept_ring2`, recorded while its radius is 2) and claims no new ring-2 tiles; tiles still flip on capture. Sprites: `CityArt` looks up `base_<base_style>_<tier>`, then `base_generic_<tier>`, then `base_generic_base` (assets/cities/<key>.png); with no art the map draws a placeholder dome scaled per tier (sea factions on a platform); factions.json has `base_style` per faction. The map label shows "City 3 (City)" (cities have no names yet) and the city panel shows the tier and "next tier: 15,000". Fog (40f) now reads the tiers from HexMap (the duplicate `tier_thresholds` in map_config.json is removed; `fog.city_bonus_tier` still names the tier). Colonies now start at 3,000 people, so the AI cannot spend a colony's people on pods or divisions until it grows (min remaining 5,000), and the AI's scout takes a capital's one queue slot first.
- Natives and fungus attrition (Task 44; docs/specs/ALIENS.md Part A, i.e. spec PRs 1 and 2; Pressure and levels are Task 45): a native nation `DivisionRoster.NATIVE_NATION` = -2 (hostile to everyone, no city, no economy, never in supply trouble, never surrenders: at 0 org it is removed). data/natives.json holds every number: Tendril Worm (60/40/12/8, move 1, fungus only, x1.25 attack when it attacks out of fungus, org damage only against a city hex) and Rust Strider (30/30/5/5, move 2, plains and hills, flees below 50% org), the spawner (no spawns before turn 3, none within 3 hexes of a city before turn 5, max_natives 6, worms 0.02 per fungus tile of density 3+ with no unit within 2, striders 0.05 per turn on a random plains tile 4+ hexes from every city), fungus placement and attrition. PM rulings: at least 2 native groups at all times (`min_natives` 2: a strider is placed whenever the count falls short, after turn 3), Striders raid (an undefended Outpost or Base next to a strider loses `raid.pop_loss` 5% of its people, at most once per 5 turns, never captured, never below 1,000 people), and fungus attrition is -5 org, -2 strength a turn and +1 move cost on fungus tiles, halved for Verdant Accord (`fungus_attrition_mult` 0.5 trait; the +1 move cost rounds down to 0), natives exempt, scouts not exempt, strength never below 1 from attrition. NativeSpawner.run (end of each turn, from main.gd) and NativeAI.raid_step; native orders are planned in DivisionRoster.run_ai through NativeAI.plan (adjacent enemy it can attack, else one random step in its habitat) and resolve in the normal simultaneous-orders step. Natives never enter or capture a city. Aliens are drawn as magenta-outlined placeholder creatures (no standee plate) and, like every enemy unit, only on Visible tiles; they own no city, so domination ignores them. There was no fungus on the map: `HexMap.place_fungus` (new games only, seeded) adds `fungus.patches` 4 patches of radius 2 away from cities (density 4 at the centre, one less per ring, drawn as a purple tint), saved in `fungus_tiles`; fungus growth and spread are still out of scope.
